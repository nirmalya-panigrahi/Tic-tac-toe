## 🚀 Overview

**ULTIMATE TIC·TAC·TOE** isn't just a game; it's a fractal battleground. Every move you make in one of the **9 nested grids** dictates where your opponent can strike next. Win three grids in a row to achieve global dominance.

## 🕹️ Game Features

- **🧠 Tactical AI**: Three levels of digital intelligence:
  - `Novice`: Basic pattern recognition.
  - `Tactician`: Advanced heuristic evaluation.
  - `Grandmaster`: Deep recursive Minimax lookahead.
- **🎨 Dual Protocols**: Toggle between **Dark Mode** (Deep Space) and **Light Mode** (Clinical Protocol) with a single command.
- **📱 Responsive Command Center**: Fully optimized for mobile deployments and desktop command stations.
- **📜 Tactical Log**: Real-time analysis of moves and strategy shifts.
- **✨ Visual FX**: Electric glow animations, blackout flicker effects, and dynamic reactive backgrounds.

## 📐 How To Play

1. **The Grid**: The board consists of 9 small grids.
2. **The Constraint**: Your move determines the specific grid your opponent must play in.
3. **Winning**: Win a small grid to mark that sector with your symbol.
4. **Victory**: Connect 3 won sectors horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
